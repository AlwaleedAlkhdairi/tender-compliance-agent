# Request for Proposals RFP-2026-014 — Campus Data Center Network Upgrade

> Synthetic dataset created for the SDAIA Academy capstone project.
> Najd Regional Utilities Company, all suppliers, figures and dates are fictional.

## 1. Introduction and Scope

Najd Regional Utilities Company (NRUC) invites qualified suppliers to submit
proposals for the supply, installation, migration and support of a complete
data center network upgrade at its Riyadh headquarters campus. The scope
covers core, aggregation and access layers across two server halls, the
replacement of end-of-life switching hardware, a new network monitoring
platform, migration of approximately 420 production servers, and three years
of maintenance and support services.

The tender reference is RFP-2026-014. Proposals must respond to every
mandatory requirement (Section 2) and address all scored requirements
(Section 3). The evaluation method is described in Section 4.

## 2. Mandatory Requirements (Pass/Fail)

Mandatory requirements are pass/fail gates. A bid that fails any mandatory
requirement is non-compliant and shall be rejected, subject to the
clarification rules in the NRUC Procurement Evaluation Guidance.

### M1 — Information Security Certification

The bidder must hold a valid ISO/IEC 27001 certification at the bid
submission deadline. A copy of the certificate, including its validity
period and certifying body, must be included in the bid.

### M2 — Local Support Presence

The bidder must operate a staffed service center inside the Kingdom of
Saudi Arabia providing 24/7 support coverage, and must name the city and
staffing level of that center in the bid.

### M3 — Comparable Project Experience

The bidder must evidence at least three (3) completed data center or campus
network projects of comparable size (400+ network endpoints or 2+ server
halls) delivered within the last five (5) years, each with a client
reference contact.

### M4 — Warranty Period

All supplied hardware must carry a manufacturer-backed warranty of at least
thirty-six (36) months from provisional acceptance.

### M5 — Delivery and Completion Time

The complete implementation, including migration of all production servers,
must be finished within one hundred and twenty (120) calendar days from
contract signature.

### M6 — Bid Validity and Bid Security

Bids must remain valid for ninety (90) days from the submission deadline and
must be accompanied by a bid security of two percent (2%) of the bid value.

## 3. Scored Requirements

Scored requirements determine the technical and financial score of compliant
bids. Each belongs to one weighted evaluation criterion (Section 4).

### S1 — Network Architecture and Redundancy (Criterion: Technical Solution)

The proposed architecture must provide full redundancy at core and
aggregation layers with no single point of failure, including redundant
power, supervisors and uplinks. Bidders must supply a network diagram and a
failure-mode description.

### S2 — Equipment Performance (Criterion: Technical Solution)

Core switching must support 100G uplinks; access switching must support 25G
server connectivity. Bidders must state offered models, port counts and
switching capacity.

### S3 — Network Security and Segmentation (Criterion: Technical Solution)

The design must implement macro- and micro-segmentation for production,
management and DMZ zones, with a documented policy model and east-west
traffic control.

### S4 — Monitoring and Management Platform (Criterion: Technical Solution)

A centralized monitoring and management platform must be offered, covering
configuration management, telemetry, alerting and capacity reporting, with
role-based access control.

### S5 — Implementation Plan and Migration Method (Criterion: Implementation and Delivery)

Bidders must provide a phased project plan with a migration method that
limits any single production downtime window to four (4) hours, including a
rollback procedure and a cutover rehearsal.

### S6 — Project Team Qualifications (Criterion: Experience and Team)

The delivery team must include at least two (2) expert-level certified
network engineers (CCIE or equivalent). CVs and certification numbers must
be included, together with the named project manager's track record.

### S7 — Support Services and SLA (Criterion: Support and Warranty)

Bidders must describe their support model for the three-year support period,
including guaranteed response times (Priority-1 response within one (1)
hour), spare-parts availability and escalation paths.

### S8 — Total Cost of Ownership (Criterion: Financial)

The financial proposal must state the total price covering supply,
installation, migration and three years of support, with a payment-milestone
breakdown. Pricing is evaluated on total cost of ownership.

## 4. Evaluation Criteria and Weights

Compliant bids are scored per criterion on a 0-100 scale and combined using
the following weights:

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Technical Solution | 40% |
| Implementation and Delivery | 20% |
| Experience and Team | 15% |
| Support and Warranty | 10% |
| Financial | 15% |

The detailed scoring method, including the financial scoring formula and
the treatment of partial compliance, is defined in the NRUC Evaluation
Criteria and Scoring Methodology document.

## 5. Submission Instructions

Bids must be submitted in a single sealed electronic package before the
deadline stated in the tender portal. Late bids are rejected. Questions may
be submitted up to ten (10) days before the deadline; answers are shared
with all bidders.
