# NRUC Evaluation Criteria and Scoring Methodology

> Synthetic dataset created for the SDAIA Academy capstone project.
> Applies to tender RFP-2026-014.

## 1. Two-Stage Evaluation

Evaluation proceeds in two stages. Stage 1 checks every mandatory
requirement (M1-M6) as a pass/fail gate: a bid failing any mandatory
requirement is declared non-compliant and receives no score, subject to the
material-deviation rules in the Procurement Evaluation Guidance. Stage 2
scores the remaining compliant bids against the weighted criteria.

## 2. Criterion Scoring Scale

Each criterion is scored on a 0-100 scale using the anchors below:

| Score band | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 90-100 | Exceeds the requirement with clear, verified evidence |
| 75-89 | Fully meets the requirement with adequate evidence |
| 50-74 | Meets most of the requirement; minor gaps or weak evidence |
| 25-49 | Partial compliance; material gaps that carry delivery risk |
| 0-24 | Fails to address the requirement or no evidence provided |

Scores must always cite the bid evidence they rely on. A statement without
supporting evidence in the bid cannot score above the 50-74 band.

## 3. Financial Scoring Formula

The Financial criterion is scored on total cost of ownership (TCO) for
supply, installation, migration and three years of support. The lowest
compliant TCO receives 100 points; every other bid receives:

    financial_score = 100 x (lowest_compliant_TCO / bid_TCO)

Abnormally low bids are handled per the Procurement Evaluation Guidance
before this formula is applied.

## 4. Weighted Total

The weighted total is the sum of each criterion score multiplied by its
weight (Technical Solution 40%, Implementation and Delivery 20%, Experience
and Team 15%, Support and Warranty 10%, Financial 15%). The highest weighted
total among compliant bids is recommended for award.

## 5. Partial Compliance on Scored Requirements

Partial compliance on a scored requirement reduces the score of its parent
criterion but does not disqualify the bid. Evaluators must record, for each
partially met requirement: what was offered, what the tender required, and
the delivery risk created by the gap.

## 6. Tie-Breaking

If two weighted totals are within one (1) point, the bid with the higher
Technical Solution score is ranked first; if still tied, the lower TCO wins.

## 7. Documentation Duty

Every score must be traceable: the evaluation record must reference the bid
document, section and quoted evidence used for each judgement, and must list
every requirement for which no evidence was found.
